## About me

I’m Falguni Sharma, a Computer Science graduate with experience across Blinkit, Zomato and MindPeers. My work brings together customer experience, AI-agent workflows, conversation design, testing and team coaching.

I focus on making customer journeys easier to follow—from understanding a request and applying the right business rules to handing over to a person with the relevant context.

## Explore the portfolio

- **Resolve:** Follow a customer-support journey through issue capture, resolution and human handover.
- **Discover:** Explore conversational recommendations guided by customer preferences.
- **Voice and chat:** Try speaking a message, listening to responses and interacting through chat.
- **Reset:** Explore interactive wellbeing activities and games.
- **Reporting:** See how session interactions and outcomes appear in a reporting dashboard.

## What this project demonstrates

Requirements mapping, conversation design, decision rules, fallback handling, workflow testing and reporting—all presented through interactive examples.

## How it works

The portfolio uses HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Voice input and playback use the browser’s Web Speech API. The demonstrations use predefined workflow logic and sample data.
